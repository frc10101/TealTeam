📖 How to fill out this form

1. Event & Team — pre-filled if your lead assigned you a robot. Only change these if your lead tells you to.

2. Alliance Color — the bumper color of the robot you're watching this match (it changes match to match).

3. Watch one robot for the whole match. Pick the closest option for each field — a best guess beats a blank. Required fields are Event, Team, Alliance Color, and Starting Position.

4. Notes — one or two specific observations. These go to your team's strategists.

5. Submit once per robot per match. Your lead scout reviews every submission before it counts.

More detail on every field is on the Help page.
