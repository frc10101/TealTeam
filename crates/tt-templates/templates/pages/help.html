Help

Scouting a robot

  1. Sign in, and pick the event at the top if it is not already showing.
  2. Open Scout. If you have an assignment, the next one is linked at the top.
  3. Fill it in while the match runs and press Save observation. What you type is kept on the device as you go, so a reload does not lose it.

The chip in the header

Synced
Everything you have entered is on the server.
Syncing…
Connected, and sending what you entered.
Offline · nothing unsent
This device can't reach the server. Everything saved before is on it.
Offline · 2 held
Forms you sent while the server was out of reach are held on this device, not saved. They are sent when the server answers; keep the page open and do not clear the browser.
1 needs review
The server refused something saved on a tablet. A lead scout decides what happens to it.

Declined

A lead scout can decline a record. The Scout page then says who declined it and why, with a Record it again button.

Server out of reach for a while

On the Scout page, open Server out of reach? and press Show the code. A lead scout who can reach the server scans it with Scan a tablet's forms, then shows you a receipt; scan it back with Scan the receipt. The tablet keeps your forms until the receipt says the server has them.

Who to ask

Your lead scout, for assignments, records and anything the chip says. An admin, for accounts and roles.
